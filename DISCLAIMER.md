# Legal and Government-Document Disclaimer

ZBiz is a document-preparation toolkit. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Thailand's Department of Business Development (DBD), Revenue Department, Social Security Office, or any other government authority.

Files in this repository are private working templates and data-validation aids. They are not official forms, certificates, approvals, legal opinions, or evidence that a company has been registered.

Before filing:

1. Obtain the current official forms and requirements from the relevant authority.
2. Verify names, identification details, registered-office evidence, share allocation, capital, objectives, and signing authority.
3. Remove example data and placeholders.
4. Obtain legal, accounting, tax, and translation review where appropriate.
5. Protect personal data and identity documents according to applicable law.

Do not add signatures, seals, QR codes, government emblems, reference numbers, or visual elements that could make a generated document appear to have been issued or certified by a government authority.
