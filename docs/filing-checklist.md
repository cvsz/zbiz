# Thailand Corporate Registration Preparation Checklist

Use this checklist to prepare a review package. Confirm current requirements directly with the DBD before filing.

## 1. Company identity

- [ ] Preferred Thai company name
- [ ] Preferred English company name
- [ ] Alternative names prepared
- [ ] Name reservation completed through the official system
- [ ] Business type confirmed
- [ ] Contact email and telephone verified

## 2. Founders and shareholders

- [ ] At least the legally required number of founders/shareholders confirmed
- [ ] Full legal names match identity documents
- [ ] Nationality and addresses verified
- [ ] Identification/passport documents are current
- [ ] Share allocation totals exactly match issued shares
- [ ] Beneficial ownership reviewed

## 3. Capital

- [ ] Registered capital confirmed
- [ ] Par value per share confirmed
- [ ] Total shares multiplied by par value equals registered capital
- [ ] Paid-up amount and timing reviewed
- [ ] Capital evidence prepared where required

## 4. Directors and authority

- [ ] Directors consent to appointment
- [ ] Director identity and address verified
- [ ] Signing authority wording approved
- [ ] Company-seal requirement decided
- [ ] Foreign-director or regulated-business implications reviewed

## 5. Registered office

- [ ] Complete Thai-format address verified
- [ ] Postal code verified
- [ ] Owner consent obtained
- [ ] Lease or occupancy evidence available
- [ ] Property-owner identification available where required
- [ ] Map, photographs, and other evidence prepared where required

## 6. Objectives and regulated activities

- [ ] Objectives match actual intended operations
- [ ] Technology, cloud, AI, consulting, training, and e-commerce activities reviewed
- [ ] Foreign Business Act implications reviewed where applicable
- [ ] Sector-specific licences identified
- [ ] Overbroad or prohibited wording removed

## 7. Meetings and resolutions

- [ ] Statutory meeting record prepared
- [ ] Director appointments approved
- [ ] Auditor appointment considered
- [ ] Share subscriptions recorded
- [ ] Registered-office and signing-authority resolutions recorded

## 8. Submission readiness

- [ ] Current official DBD forms obtained
- [ ] All names and addresses are consistent across documents
- [ ] Dates are logically consistent
- [ ] Required signatures/e-signatures are complete
- [ ] Fees and filing method confirmed
- [ ] No placeholder, sample, or redacted values remain
- [ ] Personal data stored and transmitted securely

## 9. Post-registration

- [ ] Corporate bank account process started
- [ ] Tax registrations reviewed
- [ ] VAT threshold and timing reviewed
- [ ] Payroll and social-security obligations reviewed
- [ ] Accounting records and statutory books established
- [ ] Annual filings and meeting calendar created
