# Post-Registration Checklist

> Operational checklist only. Applicability and deadlines depend on the company, its activities, employees, revenue, and current law.

## Corporate records

- [ ] Download and securely archive the official certificate and accepted registration records.
- [ ] Record the juristic-person registration number and official registered name.
- [ ] Create the statutory registers and controlled corporate-record folder.
- [ ] Confirm director signing authority and company-seal policy.
- [ ] Issue share certificates and reconcile them with the shareholder register.
- [ ] Record all filing dates, receipt numbers, and renewal obligations.

## Accounting and tax

- [ ] Appoint a qualified accountant or accounting provider.
- [ ] Confirm the accounting period and bookkeeping policy.
- [ ] Assess VAT-registration requirements and timing.
- [ ] Assess withholding-tax and corporate-income-tax obligations.
- [ ] Establish invoice, receipt, expense, and document-retention controls.
- [ ] Select an auditor where required and confirm independence.

## Banking and payments

- [ ] Prepare the bank's corporate-account opening package.
- [ ] Approve authorized signatories and transaction limits.
- [ ] Configure dual control for high-risk payments.
- [ ] Keep beneficial-owner and source-of-funds evidence available.

## Employment

- [ ] Prepare compliant employment agreements.
- [ ] Register the employer and employees where required.
- [ ] Establish payroll, leave, working-time, and personnel-record controls.
- [ ] Assess work-permit and visa requirements for foreign personnel.

## Licences and data protection

- [ ] Confirm sector-specific licences before regulated operations begin.
- [ ] Publish privacy notices and establish lawful personal-data handling.
- [ ] Restrict access to identity documents and registration evidence.
- [ ] Establish retention, backup, incident-response, and deletion procedures.

## Technology-company baseline

- [ ] Register and control company domains, DNS, and business email.
- [ ] Enable MFA for banking, email, cloud, source control, and government accounts.
- [ ] Maintain an asset and software-service inventory.
- [ ] Adopt access-control, secrets-management, backup, logging, and vulnerability-management policies.
- [ ] Review customer contracts, terms of service, acceptable-use terms, and intellectual-property assignments.
